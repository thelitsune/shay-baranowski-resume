About

Hospitality isn't her job. It's how she was raised.

Shay is an Army brat, born in Bangkok and raised on postings around the world, which meant hotels, transitions, and making strangers feel at home were part of her childhood long before they were part of her résumé. She started as a room attendant and worked her way through housekeeping and engineering before earning her first General Manager post in 1999. Over the next decade she led a widening range of properties, a French Quarter hotel, a golf resort, and a beachfront resort among them, before moving into military hospitality in 2011.

In September 2015 she personally led the opening of the Holiday Inn Express Abrams Hall at Fort Benning, GA, the very post her father was once stationed at, and the very hotel she stayed in as a kid. She grew it into a flagship military hospitality operation: a 914-room property, including one building housing 854 rooms across just four stories, making it the third-largest Holiday Inn Express in the world. Under her leadership the property earned seven consecutive years of top brand accolades, all while she oversaw roughly 66,000 guest visits a month and led her team through everything from routine turnovers to major surge operations, including coordinating lodging for 600 rooms in a single night during high-profile honor operations.

"You don't need a degree. You need to work your tail off and have compassion. If you treat your employees like gold, they'll treat your guests like gold in return."

After more than a decade leading Fort Benning's largest hospitality operation, Shay has closed that chapter, handing off a stronger team, sharper standards, and a guest-first culture built to last. She's not going anywhere geographically; she's simply ready to bring that same energy to her next business, team, or organization.
